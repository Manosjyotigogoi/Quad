# Quad — Abuse / Takedown Process

> QD-021 — Required by the Round 2 security audit. Rendered as a public
> page at `/takedown` (see `frontend/src/pages/Takedown.jsx`).

**Last updated:** 2026-08-27

## 1. Overview

Quad is a student marketplace. We take reports of abuse, fraud, and
illegal content seriously. This document describes how to report
abuse and what we do about it.

## 2. How to report

### 2.1 In-app

Every listing detail page has a **Report** button. Click it, choose a
reason, and submit. The report goes to the admin team for review.

### 2.2 Email

Email **abuse@quad.app** with:
- A link to the offending listing or user profile, **or** the listing
  ID / user ID.
- The reason for the report.
- Any supporting evidence (screenshots, etc.).

### 2.3 Emergency

If you believe you are in immediate physical danger, contact your local
emergency services first. Then notify us at **abuse@quad.app** so we
can take action on the account.

## 3. Response time

We acknowledge all reports within **72 hours** and aim to resolve
within **7 days**. Complex cases may take longer.

## 4. What we do

### 4.1 Takedown actions

Depending on the severity, we may:
- Remove the offending listing.
- Hide the listing pending review.
- Suspend the offending user's account (temporary, 7-30 days).
- Permanently ban the user.
- Revoke verification status.
- Report to law enforcement if required by law.

### 4.2 Counter-notice

If your listing was taken down and you believe it was in error, you may
submit a counter-notice to **abuse@quad.app** within 14 days. We will
review and either reinstate the listing or uphold the takedown, with a
written explanation.

## 5. Prohibited content (non-exhaustive)

| Category                          | Action                         |
|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Illegal drugs / paraphernalia     | Permanent ban + report         |
| Weapons / explosives              | Permanent ban + report         |
| Counterfeit goods                 | Takedown + warning             |
| Stolen goods                      | Permanent ban + report         |
| Hate speech / harassment          | Suspend 30 days + review       |
| Sexual content                    | Permanent ban                  |
| Personal data doxxing             | Permanent ban + report         |
| Spam / scam listings              | Takedown + suspend 7 days      |
| Academic dishonesty (homework)    | Takedown + warning             |

## 6. Anti-retaliation

We will not tolerate retaliation against users who report abuse in good
faith. Retaliation results in immediate permanent ban.

## 7. Privacy

Reports are handled confidentially. The reported user is not told who
reported them. See our [Privacy Policy](./PRIVACY_POLICY.md).

## 8. Contact

- Email: **abuse@quad.app**
- In-app: Report button on every listing / user profile.
